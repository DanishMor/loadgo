What Load 20 is

Load 20 connects customers who need goods moved with drivers and transporters who own trucks. It does not move money: payments are made directly between the parties and the app only records them.

Data we collect

  • Account: mobile number (for the one-time password login), name, role (customer, driver or transporter), language, optional e-mail, company name, GST number and addresses.
  • Drivers and transporters: driving licence number and expiry, vehicle registration number, PAN, the last 4 digits of Aadhaar (the full number is never stored), vehicle details and document expiry dates, payout UPI id. These are formats checked in the app and are not verified against government records.
  • Loads and trips: pickup and drop places, cargo details, prices and offers, trip status, delivery proofs, chat messages between the two parties of a booking, ratings and support tickets.
  • Location: the driver's approximate position while location sharing is on, and during a trip. Customers may use location once to pick the nearest city. Location is never collected in the background when the sharing switch is off.
  • Microphone: while you hold the mic button, to turn speech into text, and during an in-app call. Audio is not recorded or stored by Load 20, and call audio does not pass through Load 20 servers (it goes directly between the two phones).
  • Chat and call records: the messages of a booking, who called whom, when, and how the call ended (no audio). Messages that were stopped because they carried a phone number, a UPI id or another app are not sent; a short copy (up to 120 characters), the kind and the time are kept as a "violation", and a strike count is kept on your profile.
  • Automatic clean-up: technical error samples, unanswered assistant questions and call records carry an expiry date (about 3 months, 6 months and 1 year) after which they are deleted automatically. When you delete your account, the links you shared for a bilty (LR) are switched off.
  • Device and app data: device identifier used for fraud checks, app version, and anonymous crash and error reports.
  • Photos (when the photo switch is on): photos of papers (driving licence, RC, insurance, PUC, permit, fitness) you add for your own account and your trucks, and photos taken on a trip (at pickup, at delivery, proof of delivery, damage). Papers can be seen only by you and Load 20 admins who verify them (each look is written to an audit log). Trip photos can be seen by the two parties of the booking and admins. The app never makes a public link to a photo. Photos are removed when your account is deleted, except trip photos that belong to the other party's record of a finished trip.
  • Push notifications: a device token so we can send you alerts you allow (booking, chat, payment, offers). The message text is short and has no phone number or chat text. You can switch groups off in Settings; safety alerts stay on.
  • Earnings records: your earnings, commission, payout requests and their status are kept as records. Load 20 does not hold your money. Staff approve payouts with two people and every action is written to an audit log.
  • Online payment (only if the owner switches it on): the payment partner (Razorpay) handles card or UPI details. Load 20 does not receive or store card numbers, only the order, the amount and whether it was paid.
  • Login safety: how many login codes were asked for and how many wrong codes were typed, so that guessing can be stopped.
  • Live trip location (drivers, only if the switch is on and you agree): while a trip is on the way, your position goes to the customer of that trip only. A notification shows while it runs. The last position is removed one day after the trip ends.
  • Maps (only if the switch is on): the two cities of a route are sent through our server to Google to get the road distance and time. No name or phone number is sent.
  • Text messages (only if the switch is on): a short SMS to your own phone for trip steps when you cannot receive push, and, if you press SOS, to the emergency contacts you saved. A text-message partner delivers them. The login code stays with Firebase.
  • ID checks (only if the switch is on): a licence, RC, PAN or GSTIN number may be checked with a verification partner. Of the Aadhaar number only the last four digits are kept, and they are never sent anywhere. Admins can still review your papers by eye.
  • Analytics: nothing is collected until you agree in Settings.
  • App integrity: the app may show Google Play Integrity proof (Firebase App Check) so that our servers can tell the real app from a copy.
  • Khata and business card: your party notebook is private to you. A business card is made on your phone and shared only when you press Share; your phone number is on it only if you switch that on each time.

Why we use it

To run the service (matching, bookings, safety, support), to prevent fraud and duplicate accounts, to send in-app and push notifications you allow, and to improve the app.

Who processes it

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config) stores and processes the data on our behalf. We do not sell personal data. The other party of a booking sees the details needed for that trip (name, rating, vehicle number, verified badge and trip details). Phone numbers are never shown between customers, drivers and transporters; they chat and call inside the app.

What Load 20 staff can see. For safety, Load 20 admins can see a person's phone number (every view is written to an audit log), the call history details (who, when, how it ended; calls are not recorded) and the chat of one booking, but a chat is opened only when there is a report or a dispute about that booking (and that is logged too).

Bilty (LR) and share links. A transporter, or a customer on their own booking, can make a numbered LR. The rate, margin, phone numbers and value fields are kept apart from the public part and are readable only by the person who made it, the two parties of the booking and admins; the driver gets a driver copy without them. A share link shows only the copy type you choose, has an end date, can be revoked, and counts how many times it was opened. Making and sharing an LR, and every inspection request or approval, are recorded in an audit log.

Your choices

  • Consent switches for location, analytics and marketing are in Settings.
  • You can download your data and delete your account from Settings. Deletion is blocked while a trip is active. Bookings, invoices, ledger lines, ratings and chats of finished trips are kept for the legal retention period (see the retention table in the app documentation) and are no longer linked to a usable account.
