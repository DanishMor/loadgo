Delete your account

Draft. This text describes what the app does today and must be reviewed by a lawyer before the Play Store release. Contact details: [add support email and phone].

In the app

Open Load 20, then Profile > Settings > Delete account. Type DELETE and confirm. This removes your profile, vehicles, saved places, notifications and settings, and signs you out for good.

What is kept

Bookings, invoices, ledger lines, ratings and chat messages of finished trips are kept for the legal retention period because the other party and the tax rules need them. They are no longer tied to a usable account.

You cannot open the app?

Write to [add support email] from the registered mobile number's details (name and the last 4 digits of the number) and we will delete the account for you. Deletion is not possible while a trip is active or while the account is on hold; support will explain.
