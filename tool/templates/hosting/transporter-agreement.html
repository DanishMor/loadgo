Transporter agreement

DRAFT, vakeel se review zaroori (lawyer review needed before release). This text describes what the app does today. Contact details: [add support email and phone].

Who this is for

A person or company that owns trucks, adds drivers and vehicles to {{APP_NAME}}, and bids for or accepts loads for its fleet.

Your promises

  • Your business details (name, PAN, GST if any) are true. You own or are allowed to run each vehicle you add, and each vehicle's papers are valid.
  • You attach only drivers who agree, who hold a valid licence for that vehicle, and who know these rules. You are responsible for what your drivers do on your trips, including cancellations and delays.
  • You assign a driver and vehicle before pickup and keep the app up to date when you change them.
  • Rate, margin and party details you enter are your own records. Margin and rate fields are visible only to you, the two parties of the booking and admins as the privacy policy says.

Bilty (LR), statements and e-way bill

An LR you issue is your record and you are responsible for what it says. After issue it is not edited: a change is a new version, and a cancellation needs a reason. The e-way bill field is only a record; filing with the government portal is your job. Party and month statements are made from the app's records and should be checked against your books.

Money

Customers settle with you or your driver directly; the app records status. You keep your own accounts, GST and tax. Pending amounts by party are shown from recorded payments only.

Drivers and attached vehicles

If a driver leaves your fleet, their vehicles are detached. You must finish or hand over running trips first. You do not hold a driver's own documents longer than needed.

Action on your account

Fraud, wrong papers, repeated cancellations or safety reports can lead to a hold, suspension or ban of the fleet or of single drivers or vehicles. You can ask for a review.

What {{APP_NAME}} is not

{{APP_NAME}} is a marketplace. It does not insure goods or vehicles, does not guarantee loads, and is not a party to the transport contract between you and the customer.
