Public copy: `hosting/refund.html`. DRAFT: lawyer review needed before release.

## Money in LoadGo
LoadGo does not hold or move money yet. The customer pays the driver or transporter directly (cash or UPI) and the app records the status. So any refund is settled between the two of them; LoadGo helps by showing the record and by reviewing a dispute.

## Cancelling a load
- An open load that no driver has accepted can be cancelled at any time, free of charge.
- A scheduled booking can be cancelled free until the free-cancel time shown on the booking (usually 2 hours before pickup).
- After that, the cancel preview on the booking shows the charge before you confirm. The amount is shown in rupees and is agreed with the driver; the app records it.
- Before a driver starts loading, a driver may cancel; the load then reopens for other drivers. Repeated cancellations lower the trust score and can limit the account.

## Refunds and credits
- A charge paid directly to the driver is refunded by the driver. The booking shows what was recorded.
- LoadGo credits used on a load are recorded in the credits history. Credits have no cash value and are not paid out.

## Disputes
Damage, shortage, delay or a wrong charge can be raised from the trip screen as a claim or a dispute. Support reviews the record (timeline, proofs, chat of that booking) and gives a decision. A decision is about the record in the app; it is not a court order.
