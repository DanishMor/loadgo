# LoadGo driver agreement (DRAFT, vakeel se review zaroori)

Public copy: `hosting/driver-agreement.html`. DRAFT: lawyer review needed before release.

## Who this is for
A person who drives a truck and uses LoadGo to find loads, make offers and carry goods.

## Your promises
- You are 18 or older, hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle, and give true information. Your documents (licence, RC, insurance, permit, fitness, pollution) are valid; an expired document stops you from accepting loads.
- You carry the goods you accepted, on the agreed date, with the vehicle you named, and you do not hand the trip to someone else without telling the customer through the app.
- You use the pickup and delivery codes only with the customer or the person they name, and you never ask for or give a code outside the app.
- You do not ask for or share phone numbers, UPI ids or other apps in chat, and you do not agree to take a trip outside the app to avoid its rules.
- You keep to traffic laws, load limits and rest rules. You tell the app about an accident, a breakdown or a delay when it happens.

## Money
You and the customer settle the agreed fare directly (cash or UPI). The app records advance, balance and the confirmation of each. You confirm payment you receive, and you do not confirm payment you have not received. Detention and extra charges are agreed in the app before they count.

## Cancelling
You may cancel before loading starts; the load then reopens. Repeated cancellations lower your trust score and can limit your account. Cancelling after loading needs support.

## Safety and records
Location sharing is on only while you choose it or during a trip. Chats, call records (not audio), trip timeline and proofs are kept as the privacy policy says and can be used to settle a dispute.

## Action on your account
Wrong documents, fraud, repeated rule breaks or safety reports can lead to a hold, suspension or ban. You can ask for a review; strike appeals are heard by a person.

## What LoadGo is not
LoadGo is a marketplace. It is not your employer, does not insure the goods or the vehicle, and does not promise a number of loads or a level of income.
