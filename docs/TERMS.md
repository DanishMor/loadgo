# LoadGo terms of service (draft)

Public copy: `hosting/terms.html`. Lawyer review needed before release.

## Using LoadGo
You must be 18 or older and give true information. One mobile number is one account and one role (customer, driver or fleet owner).

## What LoadGo does
LoadGo is a marketplace that lets customers post loads and drivers make offers. The transport contract is between the customer and the driver. LoadGo does not carry goods, does not hold money, and is not responsible for the goods in transit.

## Rules
- Do not post prohibited goods or false loads, do not take trips outside the app to avoid platform rules, and do not share the one-time trip codes with anyone but the other party.
- Keep vehicle and driver documents valid. Expired documents stop a driver from accepting loads.
- Be respectful in chat. Reports and fraud signals can lead to a hold, suspension or ban.

## Prices and payments
Fares shown in the app are estimates. The agreed price is the accepted offer. Payment is made directly between customer and driver (cash or UPI); the app records it. Platform fee, GST and cancellation charges are shown before you confirm.

## Cancellation and disputes
Cancellation charges follow the cancellation policy shown in the app. Damage, shortage or delay can be raised as a claim from the trip screen; support reviews it.

## Liability
To the extent the law allows, LoadGo is not liable for loss of goods, delays, or the behaviour of users. Nothing here limits rights you have under Indian consumer law.

## Ending the service
You may delete your account at any time. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
