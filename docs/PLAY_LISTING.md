## Full description

### English
FULL_EN_START
LoadGo connects people who need goods moved with truck drivers and transporters.

For customers
- Post a load in three steps. Your last route and goods are remembered, and an unfinished load is saved as a draft.
- See the fare before you book, with a plain explanation of every part, and what a cancellation would cost at that moment.
- Get offers from drivers, compare price, rating and vehicle, and accept one.
- Follow the trip with a timeline and an estimated arrival. Share a tracking link with your receiver.
- Pickup and delivery are confirmed with a code only you hold.
- Rate the trip, report a problem or book the same route again in one tap.

For drivers and transporters
- See loads picked for your vehicle and route. A bid assistant shows a fair range and why.
- Trip steps keep working on weak networks and are sent when the signal returns.
- Payment status and the day you can expect your money are shown clearly.
- Transporters manage vehicles, drivers, parties, loading receipts (LR) and statements by month.

Safety and privacy
- Your phone number is never shown to the other side. Chat and calls happen inside the app.
- Night-trip and rest reminders, an SOS button and emergency contacts.
- Languages: English, Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Kashmiri and Urdu.

LoadGo does not take online payments yet: customer and driver settle directly, and the app records the status. During the pilot, joining may need an invite code.
FULL_EN_END
